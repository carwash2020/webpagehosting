Type a contact in once, download a .vcf file that saves straight into your phone's contacts — no re-typing.

How to Use: Contact Card Generator

Fill in whatever fields you have — only a first or last name is required.

  • Tap Download Contact Card to get a .vcf file.
  • Open that downloaded file on your phone and it'll offer to save straight into your Contacts app — no re-typing.
  • Nothing here is saved in this tool or sent anywhere; it only exists in the downloaded file.
