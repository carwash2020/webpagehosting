How to Use: Route Planner

Type in today's job addresses in the order you plan to visit them.

  • Use + Add Stop for more addresses, or the ↑/↓ arrows to reorder, × to remove.
  • Tap Open Full Route in Google Maps to open the whole route as one set of driving directions, starting from wherever you are.
  • Google Maps caps routes at 10 stops — if you hit that, use the individual "Open this stop" links below the form instead.
  • Nothing here is saved between visits — refreshing the page clears the list.
