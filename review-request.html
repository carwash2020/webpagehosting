How to Use: Review Request Sender

Use this right after finishing a job, while it's fresh.

  • Customer name and job description are both optional — the message reads naturally either way.
  • Enter the customer's phone number, then tap Open Text Message — it opens your phone's messaging app with the message pre-filled on most phones.
  • If your phone doesn't pre-fill it, tap Copy Message first and paste it in.
  • Not feeling one of the six phrasings? Tap Try Another to shuffle to a different one.
  • Nothing here is saved or sent to any server — it all happens in this browser tab and your phone's own messaging app.
