How to Use: Review Request Sender

Use this right after finishing a job, while it's fresh.

  • Customer name and job description are both optional — the message reads naturally either way.
  • Enter the customer's phone number, then tap Open Text Message — it opens your phone's messaging app with the message pre-filled on most phones.
  • If your phone doesn't pre-fill it, tap Copy Message first and paste it in.
  • Not feeling one of the six phrasings? Tap Try Another to shuffle to a different one.
  • Remind Me Tomorrow / in 2 Days clears the form and holds onto these details locally, surfacing them at the top of this page once that date arrives — useful if you'd rather not ask for a review the second you walk out the door. This only shows up if you happen to open this page again on or after that date; it does not send an actual phone notification on its own.
  • A short local log of who's been sent a request lives on this device, purely to warn you if you're about to send to the same number again within 30 days — it never leaves this browser or gets sent anywhere.
