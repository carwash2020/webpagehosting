What we fix in St. George

Not cooling

Warm fridge, produce going soft, or a unit that runs constantly. Often dusty condenser coils, a frosted evaporator, or a failed defrost part — not automatically a dead compressor.

Freezer works, fridge doesn't

That's usually airflow between the compartments: a blocked vent, a failed damper, or the fan that moves cold air from the freezer into the fridge.

Ice maker and leaks

Ice maker stopped, water pooling inside, or a puddle on the floor. Commonly the inlet valve, the fill line, or a clogged defrost drain — standard parts, not a reason to replace the whole unit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I repair this refrigerator or replace it?

Depends on the machine's age and what's actually wrong. Dusty condenser coils, a frosted evaporator, or a single failed part are usually worth fixing. A failed compressor on an already-older unit is the point where replacement often makes more sense. We diagnose it in person first, then give you an honest answer — including telling you when buying new is the better call.

Is there a trip fee for St. George jobs?

Almost never. St. George is our home base, so nearly every address in the city falls well inside the free 15-mile radius. The only exception is the far edges of town, where the standard $25 trip fee beyond 15 miles would apply, and we'll tell you upfront if it does.

Do you offer same-day service, and can you fix it the same visit?

Same-day arrival: yes, when the schedule allows — St. George jobs usually get the fastest openings because this is home base. Call or text (435) 414-1667 to check the same day rather than booking online if it can't wait. Same-visit repair: often, yes, when the part is one we already carry or can source that day. If it needs to be ordered, we'll tell you before any work starts.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes, work is guaranteed. Parts used are covered under whatever warranty the manufacturer sets, which can vary by part.

What parts of St. George and Washington County do you cover?

St. George is home base — downtown, Bloomington, Sunbrook, Little Valley, and the newer growth toward Desert Color and Divario. We also cover Washington County as standard: Washington City, Hurricane, Santa Clara, Ivins, Leeds, and La Verkin. Cedar City and Mesquite, NV are available by request.

Do you offer estimates before starting work?

Pricing is set once the problem has been diagnosed. Some jobs can be estimated over the phone, but many issues need an in-person look before a firm price can be given. Either way, you'll know the cost before any work begins.
