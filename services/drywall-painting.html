Common Questions

Can you match my existing wall texture?

In most cases, yes. Texture matching is judged in person against the actual wall, since lighting and the exact texture pattern both affect how close a match reads once it's painted.

Do you supply the paint, or do I?

Either works. If you already have paint or a specific color matched, we're happy to use it; otherwise we can source paint as part of the job.

Is a crack in my wall a sign of foundation problems?

Usually not. A single hairline crack running diagonally from the corner of a door or window is a stress crack, and it's cosmetic in the vast majority of cases. What's worth a closer look: a crack wider than about a quarter inch, one running horizontally along a wall, one with a step or offset you can feel with a fingertip, or cracks in several rooms at once along with doors and windows sticking throughout the house. That pattern points toward foundation settling, and we'll tell you to have a foundation specialist look at it instead of billing you to paint over it.

Can you patch the holes left by an old TV mount or shelf?

Yes. Anchor and screw holes get the same treatment as a door-knob dent or nail pop: filled, textured to match the wall around them, and painted so the patch doesn't stand out. If the TV or shelf is going back up somewhere else, the mounting can happen in the same visit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the crack above my door keep coming back?

It's almost always a stress crack. A door or window opening interrupts the wall, and as the house expands and contracts with St. George's summer-to-winter temperature swings, the stress collects at the top corner. Compound smeared over the crack has nothing to resist that movement, so it reopens along the same line within months. The fix that holds is cutting the crack out slightly wider, bridging it with mesh or paper tape set in compound, then feathering it out over a wider area before painting.

Do you offer estimates before starting work?

Pricing is set once the problem has been diagnosed. Some jobs can be estimated over the phone, but many issues need an in-person look before a firm price can be given. Either way, you'll know the cost before any work begins.
