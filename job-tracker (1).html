How to Use: Job Tracker

This page combines five related tools into tabs. Data lives in this browser, and also syncs to the cloud if you've set that up on the Workspace page.

  • Jobs — search by title or client name, add a job with priority/status/due date, sort by date or priority, hide completed jobs (remembered next time you visit), tap "Advance Status" to cycle Not Started → In Progress → Done, or "Photos" to attach before/after pictures (on mobile, your phone offers both the camera and your photo gallery). Tap the ☆ star on any photo to queue it for the public website's gallery with a caption — this doesn't publish it automatically, it just adds it to a review queue on the Dashboard.
  • Cost Lookup — type in hours and parts cost to see the standard price instantly, now with sales tax included in the total. Check/uncheck "Tax labor" and "Tax parts/materials" separately since Utah's rules genuinely differ by job type (not tax advice — confirm your specific situation with the Utah State Tax Commission or your accountant). Optionally type the client's name first — it checks your job history and auto-suggests whether they're new or returning (still overridable). Save frequent job types under "Price Reference" and tap one anytime to reload it.
  • Expenses — log expenses or mileage, now with vendor, payment method, and an optional link to a specific job. A receipt photo is required for expense entries (not mileage, since there's no physical receipt for driving miles) — tap "View" on any logged entry to see its receipt. Mileage rate lives right next to the Miles field since it's only relevant for mileage entries. Export CSV anytime for a backup or for taxes — now includes a link to each receipt too.
  • Contacts — a quick address book for vendors, suppliers, or recurring clients. Tap "History" on any contact to see their past jobs and lifetime spend (matched by exact name against Jobs and Invoices).
  • Notes — keep as many separate, named notes as you want. Search by title or content, click one on the left to open it, or start a new one. Saves automatically as you type.

Set up Cloud Sync once on the Workspace page's "?" help, and this data follows you to any device using the same sync code. The small dot next to the sync status shows whether live updates are connected — green means changes on another device show up here automatically, no refresh needed.
