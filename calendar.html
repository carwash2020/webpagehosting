How to Use: Calendar

This calendar only shows jobs you've specifically flagged. On the Jobs tab in Job Tracker, either check "Show this job on the Calendar tool" when creating a job, or tap "Show on Calendar" on an existing job's card to add it after the fact — not every job needs to clutter your personal calendar, so nothing shows up here automatically.

An orange dot on a day means there's a job due then. Tap the day to see details below the calendar.

"Add to Phone" downloads a small file (a `.ics` file — the universal calendar format every phone understands) for that specific job. Tap the downloaded file and your phone will offer to add it to whichever calendar app you use (Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, Outlook, etc.). This is a one-time export, not a live sync — if you change the job's date afterward, you'll need to re-export it to update your phone's copy too.
