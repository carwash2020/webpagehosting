What We Fix

Washers

Won't drain, won't spin, leaking water, or no power at all. Most of these trace back to a blockage, a worn belt, or a switch, not a dead machine.

Dryers

Runs but won't heat, takes forever to dry, loud thumping or grinding, or won't turn on. Often a single inexpensive part once we're in there.

Installation & Hookup

New washer or dryer delivered but not connected? We'll get it hooked up, leveled, and tested before we leave.

Vent Cleaning

A restricted dryer vent is the single most common reason a heating problem comes back after a repair. We check the full run while we're already there.

Common Questions

Do you work on all washer and dryer brands?

Yes, major residential brands. Parts availability varies by brand and age, which we can usually tell you same-day once we've looked at the machine.

Can you fix it the same visit?

Often, yes, when the part is one we already carry or can source that day. If it needs to be special-ordered, we'll tell you upfront and get it scheduled as soon as it's in.

Is it worth repairing, or should I just replace it?

Depends on the machine's age and what's actually wrong. We'll give you an honest answer once we've diagnosed it, including telling you when replacement is the better call.
