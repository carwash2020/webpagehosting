How to Use: Invoice Generator

Fill this out per job, right after you finish.

  • Invoice tab — fill in Invoice Details and Bill To, then add each line item (labor, parts, etc.) in the line-items table. Uncheck "Tax?" on any row that's exempt (see the note above the totals for why some rows might not be taxable). Tap Generate for a branded PDF with your Venmo QR built in.
  • Quote / Estimate tab — same idea, before a job starts, including the same tax handling. Download an estimate PDF (clearly labeled as an estimate, not a bill), or tap Convert to Invoice once the work's done to copy everything straight over to the Invoice tab instead of re-typing it — including which rows were marked taxable.
  • The sales tax rate is shared with the Job Tracker's Cost Lookup tab — change it in either place and it updates everywhere.
  • Everything generated here is saved automatically — invoices and estimates are logged on this device (and synced, if Cloud Sync is set up), and every invoice also flows into Job Tracker's Income tab. Nothing needs re-entering elsewhere.
