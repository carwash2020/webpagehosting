How to Use: Dashboard

This is the home base for the whole business — bookmark this one page.

  • Business Snapshot — Last 7 Days, This Month, All Time, or a Custom Range for pulling tax-period numbers. Revenue comes from invoices; expenses/mileage from the Job Tracker's Expenses tab. Last 7 Days also shows a comparison against the 7 days before that.
  • Outstanding Balance / Overdue — unpaid invoice totals. Overdue means past the invoice's own terms (Due Upon Receipt / Net 15 / Net 30) with no payment marked yet.
  • Job Status — how many jobs are Not Started, In Progress, and Done.
  • Top Vendors — where expense money went this period, based on the Vendor field in the Expenses tab.
  • Due Soon — jobs due in the next 7 days that aren't Done yet.
  • Invoices — every invoice you've generated. Tap the status badge on any of them to mark it Paid or Unpaid.
  • Backup & Restore — download everything (jobs, expenses, invoices, contacts, notes, price reference) as one file, or restore from a previous one. This is separate from cloud sync — a copy that lives on your device, not dependent on Supabase being reachable.
  • Tools — every internal tool, one tap away.

Data is saved directly in your browser, and quietly kept in sync across your devices in the background. Marking a job "Done" in the Job Tracker also offers to jump straight to a pre-filled review request for that client.
