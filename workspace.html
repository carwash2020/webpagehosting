How to Use: Dashboard

This is the home base for the whole business — bookmark this one page.

  • Business Snapshot — toggle between This Month and All Time. Revenue comes from invoices generated in the Invoice Generator; expenses and mileage come from the Job Tracker's Expenses tab.
  • Job Status — a quick bar breakdown of how many jobs are Not Started, In Progress, and Done.
  • Due Soon — any job with a due date in the next 7 days that isn't marked Done yet.
  • Recent Invoices — your last 5 generated invoices, most recent first.
  • Tools — every internal tool, one tap away.

Every tool has its own "?" button with instructions specific to that page. Data is saved directly in your browser, and quietly kept in sync across your devices in the background — nothing to set up or press. Marking a job "Done" in the Job Tracker will also offer to jump straight to a pre-filled review request for that client.
