SERVING WASHINGTON CITY

Handyman & Appliance Repair in Washington City, Utah

Washington City is one of the fastest-growing cities in the entire state — population climbed from around 28,000 in 2020 to nearly 39,000 by 2026. It's also one of the oldest settlements in the region: the historic pioneer core downtown, near the old Cotton Mill, was the first town built on the Virgin River. That same city now stretches out into Long Valley, Washington Fields, and Sienna Hills, some of the biggest active new-construction areas in Washington County.

From the Historic Core to Long Valley's New Builds

Established neighborhoods like Green Spring, Coral Canyon, and the homes along the Telegraph Street corridor tend to need the kind of ongoing maintenance any settled neighborhood does — appliance repair and replacement, plumbing fixes, drywall and paint work. Out in Long Valley, Washington Fields, and Sienna Hills, where new phases keep rolling out, the work leans more toward finish-out punch lists, appliance installs, and getting a new home fully move-in ready.

Distance from St. George: Washington City sits directly east of St. George along the I-15 corridor — it's essentially next door.

Common Questions

Do you charge extra to come out to Washington City?

Trip fees follow the same policy everywhere we work: free within 15 miles of St. George, $25 beyond that. If you're not sure which side of that line your address falls on, just ask when you book and we'll tell you upfront.

Do you work on appliances as well as general repairs?

Yes — appliance diagnostics and repair is a core part of what we do, alongside general handyman work like plumbing fixes, drywall and paint, and assembly/installation.

How is pricing handled?

We diagnose the issue in person first, then give you a clear price before any work starts. Nothing gets billed as a surprise on the back end.
