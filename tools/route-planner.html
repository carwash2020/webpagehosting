How to Use: Route Planner

Type in today's job addresses in the order you plan to visit them.

  • Use + Add Stop for more addresses, or the ↑/↓ arrows to reorder, × to remove.
  • Tap Open Full Route in Google Maps to open the whole route as one set of driving directions, starting from wherever you are.
  • Google Maps caps routes at 10 stops — if you hit that, use the individual "Open this stop" links below the form instead.
  • A real map fills in below the stop list showing where each stop actually is, numbered in visiting order — it takes a couple seconds per stop to locate them, so give it a moment after typing. A stop it can't find (usually a typo) is called out by name rather than silently dropped.
  • Cost Analyzer — type in the one-way distance for today's trip and it estimates round-trip fuel cost, shows your flat $25/free-within-15-miles policy for reference, and adds sales tax on top so you can see a full suggested charge. The cost-per-mile field is shared with Finance's Expenses tab, so updating it here updates it there too.
  • Nothing here is saved between visits — refreshing the page clears the stop list (the Cost Analyzer's rate defaults do persist, since they're shared with Expenses).
