POS

For a quick job that doesn't need a real invoice -- charge the client's card directly, on the spot.

If this email has paid before, their card is already saved (this happens automatically the first time anyone pays an invoice) -- you'll see a one-tap "Charge" button for it. Otherwise you'll get a normal card entry form, and that card gets saved for next time too.

Every POS sale shows up in your Income log the same way any other payment does, tagged so you can always tell it apart from an invoice.

This never creates an invoice, a quote, or a portal record of any kind -- it's just a charge and an income entry.
