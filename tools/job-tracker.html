How to Use: Job Tracker

Jobs, contacts, and notes in one place. Data lives in this browser and syncs to the cloud in the background.

  • Jobs — the main list, grouped by when (Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Next 7 days, Later), with search and status filters. On a phone each card shows what you need in the truck: Done, a Call button, Directions, and ··· for everything else (start it, photos, create an invoice, send the confirmation email, log an expense, edit, delete) — long-press a card for the same list. Tap the title to open the job's own page. Swipe a card left for a quick Done. Marking a job done asks what's next: Create invoice (the form opens filled from the job) or a review request. A finished job nobody has billed wears an orange To invoice pill, and the To invoice filter lists them all, until it's invoiced, paid another way, or marked No charge from its ··· (a warranty callback, a favor). Filed under "Done," a job shows where its money is (Invoiced, Overdue, Paid) and its profit margin once it's linked to an invoice. On a real desktop screen, this same list also shows as a sortable table — click any column header to sort by it, click again to reverse. "Photos" attaches before/after pictures; tap the star on any photo to queue it for the public website's gallery.
  • List / Board / Calendar — the same jobs three ways, remembered per device. Board puts Not Started, In Progress, and Done side by side. Calendar is a month view of every dated job (plus online bookings you haven't added yet, in purple) — tap a day to see what's scheduled, then Add to Phone to drop a job into your phone's own calendar app as a one-time export.
  • Contacts — a quick address book for vendors, suppliers, or recurring clients. Tap "History" on any contact to see their past jobs and lifetime spend (matched by exact name against Jobs and Invoices).
  • Notes — keep as many separate, named notes as you want. Search by title or content, click one on the left to open it, or start a new one. Saves automatically as you type.

Set up Cloud Sync once on the Workspace page's "?" help, and this data follows you to any device using the same sync code. The small dot next to the sync status shows whether live updates are connected — green means changes on another device show up here automatically, no refresh needed.
