How to Use: Dashboard

This is the home base for the whole business — bookmark this one page. Tap any section heading (except Tools) to collapse or expand it — your preference is remembered.

  • Today — just what's scheduled for today and not yet marked done, sorted by priority, with a tap-to-call phone number and a tap-to-open-Maps address for each. The fastest way to see what's actually in front of you without wading through the full Job Tracker.
  • Business Snapshot — Last 7 Days, This Month, All Time, or a Custom Range for pulling tax-period numbers.
  • Action Items — everything that wants a response from you, in one place: new Leads from the contact form (mark Handled or Delete), jobs Due Soon (next 7 days, not done yet), Client Follow-Ups (clients whose most recent job was 6+ months ago, with a one-tap link to a pre-filled follow-up text), and Invoices (tap the status badge to mark Paid or Unpaid). The heading shows a running count of what's waiting, even while collapsed.
  • Business Health — everything you check occasionally rather than daily: Website Gallery Queue (photos starred in the Job Tracker, review-only — nothing here is live on the website until deliberately added to the actual gallery code), Business Compliance (insurance, LLC/tax info, city licenses — the heading flags an expiring or expired policy even while collapsed), Analytics (Job Status and Top Vendors), and Backup & Restore (independent of cloud sync entirely). Collapsed by default since none of this is a daily check.
  • Tools — every internal tool, one tap away: Job Tracker, Invoice Generator, Contract Generator, Route Planner, Calendar, and a few smaller ones.

A typical day, start to finish:

  1. Check Today on the Dashboard for what's already on the schedule.
  2. New job comes in? Add it in Job Tracker — check "Show on Calendar" if you want it on your phone's own calendar app too, and use the microphone icon next to Notes to dictate instead of typing.
  3. On site, update the job's status directly from the dropdown on its card (Not Started / In Progress / Mark as Complete), and attach photos with the Photos button.
  4. Marking a job Complete offers to jump straight to a pre-filled review request for that client.
  5. Need to invoice? Open Invoice Generator and use "Link to Job" so it counts toward that job's profitability in Job Tracker's Profitability tab.
  6. Need a signed agreement? Contract Generator can capture a client's signature right on the screen if they're there in person, embedded directly into the PDF — or skip that and just print/text it for them to sign on their own.
  7. End of week: skim Action Items for anything waiting, and Business Health for anything expiring soon.

Data is saved directly in your browser, and quietly kept in sync across your devices in the background — the small dot next to the sync status turns green when live updates are connected, meaning changes on another device (or a new lead coming in) show up here automatically. The small circular arrow button next to it forces an immediate refresh if you don't want to wait.

Install this as an app: iPhone/iPad — tap the Share icon in Safari, then "Add to Home Screen." Android — tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, then "Install app." Computer — look for an install icon in the address bar. Once installed, it opens full-screen with its own icon, no browser bar.

Worth knowing: this whole suite is behind a real login now — anyone hitting these pages without signing in gets redirected to a sign-in screen first. There's exactly one account, meant to keep the data private, not to support separate logins for separate people.
