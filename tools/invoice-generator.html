How to Use: Invoices

This page opens on your invoices: who owes you, what is overdue, and what you billed this month. The forms are the tabs next to it (and the orange + opens them from any page).

  • Invoices tab — every invoice, newest first, with what is still owed on it and when it is due. Tap Owed to you or Overdue (or the chips under the search) to show only those. Tap a row (or hold it) for Mark Paid — for a cash or check payment; the client's portal is told too, so they can't pay it twice — Resend to client, Open client, Open job, or Delete. On a computer those buttons are also at the end of each row. Your quotes are listed underneath, with whether the client approved or declined on their portal.
  • From this job — open an invoice from a job (its Create invoice, Ready to invoice on the Dashboard, the Job done sheet, or by picking the job in the form) and a panel above the line items offers what's already logged: the hours on the job as Labor at your last labor rate, every receipt logged against it in Finance as a part at cost (add your markup in the price), and its mileage at your last mileage rate. Tick what to bill and tap Add; Undo puts the form back. If the client has an unbilled quote for that job, Bill the quote copies the quote's lines and discount instead, and marks the quote converted when you save.
  • New invoice tab — fill in Invoice Details and Bill To, then add each line item (labor, parts, etc.) in the line-items table. Uncheck "Tax?" on any row that's exempt (see the note above the totals for why some rows might not be taxable). Use Download PDF for a branded copy, or Send to Client to email it and post it to their portal.
  • New quote tab — same idea, before a job starts, including the same tax handling. Download an estimate PDF (clearly labeled as an estimate, not a bill), or tap Convert to Invoice once the work's done to copy everything straight over to the New invoice tab instead of re-typing it — including which rows were marked taxable.
  • Quick charge tab — for a small job that doesn't need a real invoice: enter the client's email and an amount and charge their card on the spot. If that email has paid an invoice before, their card is already saved (this happens automatically the first time anyone pays an invoice) and you'll see a one-tap Charge button; otherwise you get a normal card form, and that card gets saved for next time too, after the client types their name as an authorization. Every POS sale shows up in your Income log the same way any other payment does, tagged so you can always tell it apart from an invoice — it never creates an invoice, a quote, or a portal record of any kind.
  • The sales tax rate is shared with Finance's Cost Lookup tab — change it in either place and it updates everywhere.
  • Everything generated here is saved automatically — invoices and estimates are logged on this device (and synced, if Cloud Sync is set up), and every invoice also flows into Finance's Income tab. Nothing needs re-entering elsewhere.
