How to Use: Runway Dashboard

Tap the ? next to any section title for a short explanation of just that part. This button gives you the full picture instead.

Safe to Spend

What's coming in, what your bills take, what we recommend you set aside, and what's actually safe to spend the rest of the month.

Personal Income

Day-job wages or anything else that's not from Triple H. This is what Safe to Spend and the Runway tab measure everything else against.

Bills & Expenses

Add each bill. If it's the same amount every time, mark it Fixed. If it changes (like a power bill), mark it Variable and log the real amount each month.

Business Dashboard

Pick a month and tap "Pull from Finance" to fill in Revenue, Expenses, and Fuel automatically from what you've already logged there.

Runway Summary

Your emergency fund progress, how much the business is currently supplementing your income, a rough estimate of when the business alone could cover your bills, and a Safe Draw Calculator.

Syncing

Everything here syncs automatically with your other Triple H tools. Backup & Restore at the bottom is a separate, no-setup safety net.
