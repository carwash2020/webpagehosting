How to Use: Site Content

Edits the public website's changeable text and numbers directly, with no code push. Only accounts with the "Site content" permission can open this page or save anything from it.

  • Website text and numbers — the Google rating and review count, 2 announcement banners, the homepage hours, phone, and email. Edit any of them, press Review & publish, check each change against what's live now, then publish. Anything that doesn't look right (a rating over 5.0, a phone number missing a digit) is flagged before you can publish, and the database refuses it too.
  • Undo — the box at the top always shows the last save with an Undo this save button. Each field also keeps its recent changes, with a button to put back any earlier value. An undo is refused, not forced, if someone has changed that field again since.
  • Content edit history — every past change to those fields, the FAQ, and Terms: who made it, and when -- a real audit trail from a database trigger.
  • FAQ editor — add, edit, reorder, or remove questions on the site's FAQ section. Review & publish lists every added, changed, removed, and moved question before anything goes live; a blank question or answer is flagged instead of silently deleting it; and Undo this save puts the whole list back the way it was.
  • Terms & Conditions editor — the same, for the sections of the Terms document.
