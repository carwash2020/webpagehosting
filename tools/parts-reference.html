Parts & Reference

A running list of common appliance issues you've actually seen, paired with the part that was usually the fix and roughly what it cost. The goal is speed — "customer says the washer won't spin" should get you to "probably the drive belt, roughly $20–30" in a few seconds, without digging through old invoices or your memory.

This isn't a substitute for actually diagnosing the job in person — it's a fast starting point based on what's been true before, not a guarantee of what's wrong this time.

The reference link, if you add one, points to wherever you actually found the part — a manufacturer's page, PartSelect, RepairClinic, or similar. It opens in a new tab; nothing is copied or stored from that page itself, just the link to it.

Use the search box to filter by symptom or part name, or tap an appliance chip to narrow the list down first.
