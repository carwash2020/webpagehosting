Parts & Reference

Look up an appliance by type or brand, and see everything for it in one place — a link to its manual, a link to its parts catalog, and any specific issues you've personally logged for it.

Tap a type chip to narrow the list, or search directly. Tap any appliance card to open it, see both links at the top, and add issues as you actually run into them on real jobs.

Every link here points to a manufacturer's own site or a real parts retailer — nothing from those pages is copied or stored here, just the link to it. A few links go to a brand's general parts catalog rather than one exact model, since that depends on the specific model number.

This isn't a substitute for actually diagnosing the job in person.
