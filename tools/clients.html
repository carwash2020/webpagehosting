Clients

Everything about the client-facing portal, split out of Dev Tools onto its own page (2026-09-03) since it had become a daily operational tool, not a diagnostics screen.

Portal accounts -- who has portal access, and a way to send or resend an invite to any email, whether or not they already have an invoice on file.

Portal invoices -- every invoice actually sent to a client, with its real paid status and a way to resend the notification email.

Portal work orders -- every "Request Work" submission, with photos, urgency, and status. Closed requests hide by default; Export CSV downloads everything for your records.

Email list -- who gets emailed the moment a client submits a new work request. Add a new hire here and they start getting notified immediately, no deploy needed.

Portal bug reports -- problems clients have reported directly from the portal's own "Report a problem" link.
