Meet Steven Robinson

Triple H Enterprises is an independent handyman and appliance repair business based in St. George, Utah, founded and run by Steven Robinson. Right now, when you call, you're talking directly with Steven, not a call center or a rotating cast of subcontractors.

That means fewer surprises and real accountability. Steven's name is on the business, and on every job it takes on.

Outside of work, Steven is a father of three. That same sense of responsibility carries over into every job: showing up on time, doing it right, and treating your home the way he'd want his own family's home treated.

Triple H Enterprises is also a registered Utah LLC. Steven is also an Eagle Scout, which is where a lot of that same show-up-and-do-it-right habit started.

Where the experience comes from

Before starting Triple H, Steven spent more than a decade working as a maintenance technician and, later, maintenance manager across industrial and commercial sites: food and beverage manufacturing plants, a large warehouse distribution center, and car wash facilities. That work meant diagnosing and repairing motors, pumps, valves, and electrical systems on equipment that couldn't afford much downtime, on a schedule of real preventative maintenance, not just fixing things after they broke.

Along the way he picked up a genuinely broad set of hands-on skills: electrical and mechanical troubleshooting, pump and valve rebuilds, welding and fabrication (stick, MIG, and TIG), and hands-on time with industrial control systems. He holds an Automation Technician certificate, and is trained and certified in lockout/tagout, forklift, and scissor lift safety. He also spent time as a Certified Nursing Assistant, work that's a world away from repair, but the same thing was true there as it is on every job now: showing up reliably and treating people's homes and routines with real care.

What that means for your repair

A washer, dryer, dishwasher, or fridge is, underneath the cabinet, the same kind of thing Steven spent years keeping running in much bigger, less forgiving environments: motors, pumps, valves, and electrical controls. That background is exactly why Triple H can usually diagnose what's actually wrong instead of guessing, and why a repair holds instead of coming back a month later.
