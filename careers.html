Part-Time Handyman Helper

Triple H Enterprises is looking for one part-time helper to join Steven on handyman and appliance repair jobs around St. George and Southern Utah. This isn't a punch-a-clock job — it's built around a flexible, choose-your-own-hours schedule and pay per completed job, so the work fits around your life instead of the other way around.

What the schedule actually looks like

There's no fixed shift. Jobs get offered as they come in, and you pick which ones fit your week — a few hours here and there, one day a week, or several, whatever works for you right now. Good for someone who wants real, flexible part-time work without committing to a set schedule.

Pay

Pay is per completed job, not hourly — most jobs land in the $35–$100+ range depending on the size and complexity of the work, and you'll know what a job pays before you take it. The more efficient and capable you are, the better that works out per hour on the clock.

What you'd be doing

  • Helping diagnose and fix washers, dryers, dishwashers, and other household appliances
  • General handyman work — drywall patches, fixture swaps, assembly, and small repairs
  • Driving to job sites around St. George, Washington City, Hurricane, Santa Clara, and Ivins
  • Representing Triple H professionally in a customer's home

What we're looking for

  • Basic repair knowledge — appliances, plumbing, drywall, or general handyman experience
  • A valid driver's license and reliable transportation
  • Your own basic hand tools (or a real willingness to build up a set)
  • Comfortable lifting and moving appliances and materials, sometimes 75+ lbs with help
  • A smartphone, for job details, photos, and texting updates
  • Reliable and on time — when you take a job, the customer is counting on you showing up
  • Comfortable being in a customer's home and representing the business well
  • Based in or near St. George, Utah
  • Willing to pass a background check

Questions About This Job

Is this a full-time position?

No — this is a single part-time helper role, built around a flexible schedule where you choose which jobs to take.

Do I need to already own tools?

Basic hand tools are expected, but this isn't a dealbreaker on its own if you have real repair experience and are willing to build up a set. Bring it up when you reach out.

Do I need certifications or a license?

No formal certification is required — basic, practical repair knowledge and a willingness to learn matter more here than a piece of paper.

How do I get paid?

Pay is per completed job rather than hourly, and you'll know what a job pays before you take it on.

How do I apply?

Call or text (435) 414-1667, or email steve@triplehenterprisesllc.biz with a bit about your background and repair experience.
